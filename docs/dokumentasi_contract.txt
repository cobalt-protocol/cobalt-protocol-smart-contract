Cobalt Protocol Smart Contracts Documentation

1. CertificateCompetition
Contract Title: CertificateCompetition
Contract Address: 0x2FEDA53447837562975568Bc8f991C2BC1282e72
Short Description: An ERC-721 NFT smart contract handling digital certificate minting for general participants and competition winners. Certificate claims require cryptographic verification of off-chain signatures from the official platform signer and can only be executed after the designated claim schedule.

2. CertificateManager
Contract Title: CertificateManager
Contract Address: 0xc0f15FFe4DB8aa49BA1e4E8992a69d45bD4be84F
Short Description: A registry smart contract that records certificate claim histories for participants and winners to prevent duplicate claims.

3. CompetitionManager
Contract Title: CompetitionManager
Contract Address: 0x2938eabf29e9F7ecaff7E11ca9794DFa904e78D7
Short Description: The core smart contract managing the complete competition lifecycle, including creation, platform fee collection, prize escrow locking, winner assignment, and automated prize payouts.
